# LATS Earth — Data Sources

LATS Earth uses publicly accessible data services for demonstration and situational-awareness features.

- Open-Meteo: weather and forecast data. See https://open-meteo.com/
- openSenseMap: public environmental sensor observations. See https://opensensemap.org/
- OpenStreetMap / Nominatim: place search and geographic data. See https://www.openstreetmap.org/ and https://nominatim.org/

Availability and coverage depend on the external services. LATS should not be treated as an official emergency-warning authority.
